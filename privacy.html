glink-teresa privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

  • Who can use it: only Google accounts invited by the owner.
  • What we receive from Google sign-in: your email address, name and profile picture. Nothing else.
  • What we store: your account details and your chat history, in a MongoDB Atlas database in the EU.

  • What we never do: sell, share or use your data for advertising.
  • Deletion: email eduard.guiu@gmail.com and your account and chats will be deleted.
